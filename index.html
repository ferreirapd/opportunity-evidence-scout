Opportunity Evidence Scout

Opportunity Evidence Scout is a private, limited-scale, read-only market research prototype.

Subject to Etsy's approval, it will use only Etsy Open API v3 to query a small number of public active listings and generate aggregate supply and price indicators.

Operation and scope

  • Manually triggered at most once per week.
  • One API request per configured search term.
  • Up to 10 listings per term, without pagination.
  • No webpage scraping or browser automation.
  • No OAuth or private Etsy member data.
  • No listing images or full descriptions.
  • No order, buyer, message, credential, or contact data.
  • No resale or redistribution of Etsy data.
  • No use of Etsy data to train AI or machine-learning models.

Privacy and data retention

The application requests only the minimum public listing fields required for its approved purpose: listing and shop identifiers, title, URL, price and currency, taxonomy, tags, listing type, number of favorers, and creation or modification timestamps.

Workflow artifacts automatically expire after 14 days. Raw Etsy content is not published. Aggregate indicators may be retained for internal evaluation.

This website has no account system, forms, advertising trackers, or application analytics.
